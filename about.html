Our Foundation

Founded in 2017, Prospera Textil is a family-owned apparel manufacturer rooted in Peru's textile heritage. With expertise in product development, manufacturing, and quality management, we provide full-package solutions for international brands. Backed by WRAP Certification and a strategic presence in Miami through Prospera 3PL, we are committed to delivering exceptional quality while building long-term partnerships based on trust, innovation, and consistency.
